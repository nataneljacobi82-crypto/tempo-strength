Four ways to train

Every class is coached. Every program is progressive. Pick one lane or rotate through all four.

01/ 04
Strength

Keep scrolling

01

Strength

Barbell-focused training built on progressive overload. Squat, press, hinge and pull, coached in groups of eight so nobody gets lost in the crowd.

A session looks like

  • Ten minutes of barbell warm-up and movement prep
  • One main lift, working up to a coached top set
  • Two accessory blocks, logged in the app
02

Conditioning

Forty-five minutes of intervals on sleds, rowers and assault bikes. Scaled to your engine, never to the person next to you.

A session looks like

  • Heart-rate capped warm-up on the bike or rower
  • Three to five interval blocks with coached pacing
  • Scores tracked week to week in the app
03

Olympic Lifting

Snatch and clean and jerk technique for every level, from your first PVC pipe to a competition total. Small platforms, lots of eyes on your bar path.

A session looks like

  • Position drills from the floor, the hang and the blocks
  • Percentage work off your current training max
  • Video review of your heaviest set before you leave
04

Mobility

Thirty minutes of joint prep, flexibility and recovery work. The class that makes the other three possible.

A session looks like

  • Hips, ankles and thoracic spine, every single session
  • Loaded stretching and controlled articular rotations
  • Breathing and down-regulation to finish
